# ⚔️ TypeFight

**Every word is a weapon. Every sentence is a battle.**

TypeFight is a single-player typing game that turns typing practice into an interactive combat experience. Fight against five increasingly challenging opponents, improve your typing speed and accuracy, build combos, and defeat the final boss.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced typist, TypeFight makes practising your typing skills more engaging.

## 🎮 Features

- **Learn with Fun:** Battle through five unique fighters, each with increasing difficulty.
- **Three Difficulty Levels:** Choose between Basic, Medium, and Advanced typing challenges.
- **Typing Speed Test:** Test your typing speed with dedicated 1-minute and 3-minute challenges.
- **Performance Tracking:** Monitor your WPM, accuracy, mistakes, and overall score.
- **Theme Switching:** Choose between two carefully designed colour themes:
  - 🌌 Midnight Slate
  - ☁️ Porcelain

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open TypeFight and start a new game.
2. Choose your preferred difficulty.
3. A sentence will appear on the screen.
4. Type the sentence as accurately and quickly as possible.
5. Complete sentences to attack your opponent.
6. Build combos and land critical hits by maintaining perfect accuracy.
7. Defeat all five fighters to complete the campaign.

You can also choose the Speed Test mode to measure your typing speed over 1 or 3 minutes.

## 🏆 Difficulty Levels

| Difficulty | Description |
|---|---|
| Basic | Short, lowercase everyday words and simple sentences. |
| Medium | Complete sentences with capital letters and punctuation. |
| Advanced | Longer sentences with numbers, quotes, and challenging punctuation. |

## ⚙️ Built With
